# Project thumbnail artwork

Generated using the built-in image generator on 2026-10-22 at Leo's request.
Editorial covers, not official logos or product screenshots. No live apps or
private data were accessed. Detail pages and the PDF retain real screenshots
except the employer LMS, which now uses an explicitly illustrative cover.

Optional `thumbnail` controls cards only, falling back to `cover` when absent.
Exports are 1280 x 800 WebP files under `public/images/projects/covers/`.

## Direction and prompts

Shared prompt: Landscape 16:10 editorial software portfolio cover. Deep navy
#101d32, off-white forms, muted blue accent. Flat print-like artwork, negative
space, strong silhouette readable at 300px. No gradients, glow, stock 3D,
robots, sparkles, grids, fake screenshots, people, statistics, or company logos.
Conceptual artwork, not a product UI or official logo.

- Employer LMS: assessment sheet, answer circles, grading check and course
  folder; muted ochre accent. Edited with the built-in generator to replace
  only the title with 'Learning Management System' on three lines, preserving
  the composition and palette, with no company name or logo. Final asset:
  `public/images/projects/covers/learning-management-system-v2.webp`.
- TEHA: interlocking message shapes becoming a checked task. Sans-serif title
  'TEHA', smaller 'Telegram Handler'. No Telegram logo.
- Lexicon: offset vocabulary flashcards with serif 'Aa', sage accent and large
  serif title 'Lexicon'.
- Multi-Tenant LMS: three separate upright course folders, shared spine;
  sans-serif title 'Multi-Tenant LMS'. No server rack or network diagram.

Navy follows Leo's palette. Subject-specific shapes identify the real project
domains without inventing UI. Large text supports thumbnail recognition;
Lexicon's serif treatment reflects its vocabulary focus. Existing layout and
ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1 remain unchanged.
